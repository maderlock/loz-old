Solo Fireshow

A captivating spectacular show! Loz combines fun banter with simply astonishing fire skills to present a high-impact display that keeps the audience engaged throughout.

Looking for a big finish to your event? Check out Loz’s astonishing finale! A 5x5ft flaming heart, balanced on a pole on Loz’s chin!

Loz can add two flaming initials into the centre of the heart – perfect for weddings or valentines events. Loz can also rig stage pyrotechnics to fire fountains of sparks from the flaming heart sculpture – not to be missed!
